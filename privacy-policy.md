# Privacy Policy — PETFORGE

**Effective date:** October 2026

PETFORGE ("the App") is a virtual-pet game. Your privacy matters, so here is the complete policy:

## Data collection: none

The App **does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data**. Specifically:

- **No accounts.** You never sign up or sign in.
- **No analytics.** We do not track usage, behavior, or diagnostics.
- **No advertising.** There are no ads and no ad trackers.
- **No network requests.** After installation, the App works fully offline and makes zero network calls.
- **On-device only.** Your pet, progress, and settings are stored only in your device's local storage. Deleting the App deletes everything.

## Children

The App is a casual game suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will ship with the App update.

## Contact

Questions: reply to the App Store listing contact for the developer.
